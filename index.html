1. Overview & Architecture

Welcome to BrainRush (“we”, “our”, or “the app”), developed and published by Tribus Interactive. BrainRush provides offline-first cognitive mini-games designed to train quick mental math, memory recall, spatial orientation, reaction speed, and focus.

We believe in strict privacy-by-design. Unlike traditional online games that compel players to create cloud accounts, submit social logins, or transmit gameplay logs to proprietary servers, BrainRush operates on an offline-first architecture. We do not operate user registration servers, nor do we build player behavioral profiles.

Core Principle: Your high scores, training streaks, custom profile names, and game preferences belong to you and are stored exclusively on your device.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

To maintain absolute clarity for our users and satisfy Google Play Data Safety requirements, the following categories of data are never collected, accessed, or stored by BrainRush:

Data Category Collection Status Explanation
Personal Identification ❌ Never Collected We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, or passwords.
Contacts & Communications ❌ Never Accessed No access to your address book, call history, or SMS messages.
Location Data ❌ Never Accessed No access to GPS, precise location, or coarse location coordinates.
Photos, Media & Files ❌ Never Accessed No storage scanning, photo library access, or file system monitoring.
Audio & Camera ❌ Never Accessed No microphone or camera hardware access.
Biometrics & Health ❌ Never Collected No physiological, fitness, or biometric data is recorded.

3. Data Stored Locally On Your Device

BrainRush saves gameplay progression and app configuration directly to your device’s internal sandboxed storage via the Android SharedPreferences mechanism. This information never leaves your phone unless you manually take a screenshot or share via the system share sheet:

  • Player Profile: Nickname, selected avatar emoji/icon, total XP earned, and unlocked player rank.
  • Cognitive Stats: Calculated Brain Score, best scores per game mode, accuracy rates, and total games completed.
  • Daily Challenge & Streaks: Current daily streak counter, dates of completed daily playlists, and streak milestones.
  • Achievements: Progress counters and unlocked badges.
  • App Preferences: Sound effect volume/mute, haptic feedback toggle, dark mode/high contrast theme, and dynamic launcher icon selection.

This data is stored purely within the application’s private sandbox. Other apps on your device cannot read or modify this data.

4. Third-Party Services & Network Usage

While the core game runs entirely offline, BrainRush integrates trusted third-party SDKs to deliver advertisements, verify software updates, and ensure crash stability when an active internet connection is available. These service providers handle data according to their respective privacy policies:

A. Google AdMob (Advertising)

We utilize Google AdMob to display non-intrusive interstitial ads between sessions and optional rewarded ads (e.g., bonus XP). Google may collect and process device technical identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID — GAID), IP address, and ad interaction metrics to deliver relevant advertisements and combat ad fraud.

Review Google’s Advertising Policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

B. Google Firebase (Diagnostic Telemetry)

BrainRush is configured with Google Firebase Core. If diagnostic telemetry is enabled, Firebase receives anonymous crash diagnostics and aggregate system events (e.g., app launch, device model, operating system version) to identify app freezes and crashes across diverse Android device configurations.

Review Firebase Privacy Information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

C. Shorebird (Over-The-Air Patch Delivery)

To rapidly deploy bug fixes and security hotfixes without requiring a full manual store update, BrainRush includes Shorebird CodePush. Shorebird periodically checks whether an approved security or gameplay hotfix is available for your app build. This communication transmits standard device architecture details (e.g., CPU architecture and current patch number) to verify update compatibility.

Review Shorebird Privacy Terms: https://shorebird.dev/privacy

5. Android Permissions Justification

BrainRush requests only the minimal technical permission required to operate connected features:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Allows the application to communicate over the network for Google AdMob ad delivery, Firebase crash diagnostic reporting, and Shorebird OTA patch validation.

Note: BrainRush functions smoothly offline. If your device is offline or in Airplane Mode, all games, high scores, daily challenges, and settings remain 100% accessible without any crashes or degraded functionality.

6. Advertising & User Choices (AdMob)

You retain full control over advertising identifiers and ad personalization directly through your Android device settings:

  • Reset or Delete Advertising ID: Navigate on your device to Settings > Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads). Here you can choose to “Reset advertising ID” or “Delete advertising ID” entirely.
  • Personalized Ads Opt-Out: You can turn off personalized advertising. When opted out, you may still see the same quantity of ads, but they will be contextual rather than personalized based on your browsing interests.

7. Data Retention & Deletion Policy

In accordance with Google Play developer policies and global data protection standards:

How to Delete Your Data

Because BrainRush stores your gameplay profile and settings strictly on your local device, you can completely and permanently delete all your data at any time without submitting a support ticket:

  1. Open your device’s Settings app.
  2. Go to Apps or Application Manager > BrainRush.
  3. Tap Storage & Cache.
  4. Tap Clear Storage (or Clear Data).

Uninstalling the BrainRush app will also permanently delete all local game files and preferences from your device.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

BrainRush is designed as an all-ages casual cognitive training application suitable for general audiences. The app does not target, solicit, or knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

If a parent or guardian becomes aware that their child has provided personal information or interacted with third-party networks through our app without consent, please contact us immediately at support@brainrush.app, and we will take immediate steps to remove any associated information.

9. Regional Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

A. European Economic Area (EEA) & UK (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), users residing in the EEA and UK have the right to:

  • Request confirmation of whether personal data is being processed.
  • Request deletion of personal data processed by third-party SDKs.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority (DPA).

B. California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

  • We Do Not Sell Personal Information: BrainRush does not sell, rent, or lease personal information to third parties in exchange for monetary consideration.
  • Right to Know & Delete: You have the right to request disclosure of categories of data collected and request deletion of personal information.

10. Security Measures

We employ strict security practices to safeguard data:

  • Transport Layer Security (TLS): Any network communication with third-party services (AdMob, Firebase, Shorebird) is enforced over encrypted HTTPS / TLS 1.3 channels.
  • Local Isolation: Game data is sandboxed within Android OS application security boundaries.
  • Code Optimization & Obfuscation: Release binaries are compiled with ProGuard and R8 minification to prevent reverse engineering and ensure software integrity.
